# Competitive Workflow Matrix

Scope: current public product behavior, not marketing-positioning alone. Evidence is first-party unless marked otherwise.

| Product | Observable workflow | Strongest capabilities | Weakness / gap relevant to us | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Taplio | surface ideas/viral posts → generate in user's voice → coach on when to publish | blank-page removal, niche inspiration, voice-oriented writer, LinkedIn focus | public workflow still optimizes primarily around post production/growth; deeper primary-source technical research and evidence provenance are not the center of the described workflow | first-party-public |
| Supergrow | capture topic/URL/PDF/video/voice note or guided interview → apply Content DNA → create 3–5 drafts → review/approve → schedule → analytics/weekly report | adaptive interview, detailed identity/voice model, knowledge base, multi-post extraction, approval board, media, analytics, MCP workflow | very strong direct competitor; technical evidence quality/provenance and claim-level source discipline are not the defining public workflow | official-doc / first-party-public |
| AuthoredUp | write/format/preview → save/schedule/publish via LinkedIn integration/extension → collect analytics → compare/reuse posts | editor quality, LinkedIn-native formatting, historical post analysis, compare two posts, reuse, best-time and format analysis | less focused on autonomous research and research-backed generation; public docs emphasize editing/analytics more than source-grounded content discovery | official-doc / first-party-public |
| Typefully | brainstorm/rewrite/expand with voice-aware AI → review in calendar/team flow → preview → publish → analytics | clean multi-network writing UX, voice-aware assistant, previews, calendar, team collaboration, LinkedIn PDF carousels, API/agent skills | general social publishing platform rather than technical-research-first creator system | first-party-public |

## Competitive conclusion — not yet product thesis

The market already covers most of the obvious surface area:

- blank-page assistance
- voice imitation/profiles
- repurposing
- scheduling
- approval
- analytics
- inspiration feeds
- AI rewriting

Therefore **“AI LinkedIn post generator with research and scheduling” is not a sufficient differentiation**.

The evidence suggests our differentiator must be evaluated around a harder workflow:

**technical discovery → primary-source understanding → author point of view → claim/evidence graph → artifact/code/diagram/benchmark → single post or ordered series → review → publish → learn.**

That is still a hypothesis until user-feedback and donor-audit stages are complete.
